# CRM Sales Pipeline Analysis

## Objective
This project analyzes CRM data to evaluate sales pipeline performance and identify key factors driving lost deals.

---

## Key Metrics
- Total Leads: 513  
- Opportunities: 867  
- Conversion Rate: 33%  
- Close Rate: 58%  

## 1. Pipeline Health & Lead Distribution

![Pipeline Health](Dashboard_Pipeline_Health.pdf)

### Key Questions
- What is the overall conversion rate from Lead to Customer?
- Where is the bottleneck in the sales funnel?
- Where are leads concentrated (by industry and country)?

---

### Insights

- The overall **Lead → Customer conversion rate is 33%**, indicating that only 1 out of 3 leads successfully converts into a customer  

- The main bottleneck occurs at the **Opportunity → Customer stage**, where conversion drops significantly (only ~20%), suggesting issues in deal closing or late-stage negotiation
  -> Focus on improving late-stage conversion by enhancing follow-up processes, refining sales strategy, and strengthening deal-closing techniques
  
- Lead distribution shows strong concentration in industries such as:
  - **Transportation & Logistics (528 leads)**
  - **Banking & Finance (404 leads)**
  - **IT & IT Services (387 leads)**  
  → These represent the largest sources of potential revenue  

- In terms of geography:

  - **Italy** generates the largest share of leads (~21%), making it the key volume market  
  - **Belgium, Netherlands, and Portugal** show higher conversion efficiency (Conversion Index >130%), indicating strong potential for scaling  
  - **Germany and Switzerland** underperform in conversion, suggesting issues in targeting or sales execution  
  → Focus on scaling high-efficiency markets, maintain performance in Italy, and optimize strategy in underperforming regions

## 2. Lost Opportunity Analysis

![Lost Opportunity](Dashboard_Lost_Opportunity.pdf)

### Key Questions
- Why are we losing deals? Which factors (industry, product, or geography) contribute most to failure?  
- What common characteristics can be observed among lost opportunities?

### Insights

- Lost rate does not vary significantly across **countries and product types**, suggesting these are not the primary drivers of deal failure  
→ Focus less on geographic or product segmentation and prioritize other factors  
- Industries such as **Agriculture & Mining (~70–80%)** show significantly higher lost rates  
→ Reassess targeting and positioning in these industries  
- This indicates a **product-market fit issue**, where the solution is not well aligned with industry-specific needs  
→ Adapt product offering and value proposition to better match industry requirements  
- The current sales approach is too **generic**, while certain industries require tailored strategies  
→ Shift from a one-size-fits-all model to **industry-specific sales approaches**  

## 3. Sales Performance & Forecast

![Forecast](Dashboard_Forecasting.pdf)

### Key Questions
- What is the expected revenue from the current pipeline over the next 3–5 months?  
- Are sales overly optimistic about expected close dates?

### Insights

- The expected revenue from the current pipeline is approximately **$244K over the next 3–5 months**, based on open deals and historical win rate  
- Projected revenue shows a **declining trend over time**, indicating that most value is concentrated in the near term and pipeline depth may be limited  
- Actual close dates are consistently **earlier than expected** average  135 days
